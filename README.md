# 🤝 Favour Economy

### The social ledger for human favours.

**No money. Just reciprocity.**

Favour Economy is a playful **social reciprocity platform** that helps communities track and visualize everyday favours without assigning them a monetary value.

You helped someone move.  
Someone covered your shift.  
You lent your charger.  
Someone sent you notes.

These things aren't transactions, but they still matter.

Favour Economy makes that invisible exchange of help **visible, understandable, and fun.**

> **You've helped Rahul 5 times. Rahul has helped you 0 times. Maybe it's time to ask for a favour. 👀**

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **Favour Tracking** — Log everyday acts of help and categorize them.
- ⚖️ **Reciprocity Tracking** — See how balanced your relationships are.
- 👀 **One-Sided Relationship Detection** — Identify relationships where one person is consistently giving more.
- 🙋 **Favour Requests** — Ask your community for help.
- 🌐 **Favour Map** — Visualize how favours move across the community.
- 💡 **Smart Insights** — Get playful insights about your kindness and relationships.
- 🏆 **Gamification** — Earn badges and community recognition.
- 🎟️ **Favour Receipts** — Turn completed favours into fun shareable receipts.
- 🌙 **Dark & Light Mode** — Fully responsive modern UI.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Built for desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## 🧠 The Core Idea

Favour Economy isn't about putting a price on friendship.

It's about answering a simple question:

> **"Are we showing up for each other?"**

The platform treats reciprocity as a **social signal, not a debt**.
